Exhibit A: Statutory Compliance & Legal Framework

Federal tax law, IRS regulations, FTC Safeguards, and evidentiary standards

← Operations Triage

1. IRS Publication 1345 § 5 — Electronic Signature & KBA Governance

Remote Form 8879 execution mandates out-of-wallet identity verification. Three failed attempts require statutory wet-signature fallback on paper Form 8879. Browser-only signing simulations are strictly non-authoritative.

2. Treasury Department Circular 230 § 10.22 — Due Diligence

Licensed practitioners must exercise independent diligence in verifying taxpayer records. Document acceptance and rejection must be committed by authorized staff on the trusted backend with contemporaneous reviewer notes.

3. FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR § 314.4) — Customer Data Protection

Mandates Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all personnel accessing customer financial information, encrypted document storage vaults (AES-256), and tamper-evident audit logs.

4. IRC § 7216 & Treas. Reg. § 301.7216-3 — Confidentiality

Strict criminal penalties for unauthorized use or disclosure of tax return information. Requires affirmative, knowing, and voluntary taxpayer consent logged with non-repudiable audit evidence.
